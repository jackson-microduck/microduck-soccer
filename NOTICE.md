# Microduck model attribution

The robot meshes in `public/assets/microduck.glb` and the joint layout in
`public/assets/kinematics.json` are adapted from the Microduck model published
by Pollen Robotics.

- Source: https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl
- Simulator export used as the web mesh source: https://huggingface.co/spaces/pollen-robotics/microduck-simulator
- Copyright: Pollen Robotics
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes made for this fan game: internal electronics and tiny bearings were
left out of the rendered rig, materials were recolored, and the meshes are
posed with original gameplay animations. This project is non-commercial.

If you share the game or the meshes, keep this credit, say the files were
modified, and share the adaptation under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
